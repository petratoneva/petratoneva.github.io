About

As an interdisciplinary researcher and cultural advocate with a lifelong passion for both science and art, I work across disciplines to make the invisible visible. Using geospatial analysis, machine learning, and statistical modelling in R, Python, and MySQL, I transform complex data into meaningful insights across diverse fields, from invasive species monitoring and the assessment of sustainability indicators to the study of intracellular protein localisation.

I am immensely proud of my work with the Luminary Cinema Foundation, which I co-founded in 2024 to address the cultural isolation of rural communities across Bulgaria, where I am from. Since its founding, our non-profit has organised more than 120 film screenings across over 20 villages, has developed and delivered creative workshops for children, and has created a participatory and decentralised film network.

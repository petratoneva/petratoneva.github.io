Cultural Work

Through the Luminary Cinema Foundation and our Cinema in the Village (На кино, на село) initiative, I work to decentralise access to cinema, support rural communities, and develop participatory cultural programmes. As Executive Director, I oversee grant writing, project management, strategic development, fundraising, partnership building, and programme implementation, working with local cultural centres and community organisers to bring cinema and cultural activities to underserved communities across Bulgaria.
